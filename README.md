# Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer

**🏆 7th place, NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026** (team SHEclipse)

A 3D space adventure for kids aged 7 to 12, in English and Arabic. Kids learn what it takes to reach Mars and live there. Nuri, a small alien helper, gives hints but never solves a task for them.

**Play:** https://junior-astronaut-sheclipse.netlify.app/junior-astronaut/
(Best on an iPad or a laptop.)

## The missions

- **Prologue:** a mysterious invitation and a spaceship flight.
- **Level 1: Atacama Desert.** Fix the map, explore the driest desert on Earth, and see why scientists use it to study Mars.
- **Level 2: Training Academy.** Plan the route to Mars, suit up, pack, check the systems, and handle an emergency.
- **Level 3: Into Space.** Pick a safe seat position, launch, catch floating objects in microgravity, and aim the antenna at Earth.
- **Level 4: Life on Mars.** Keep the base alive: oxygen, water, food, power, shielding and crew. Real NASA solar-storm data sets the daily risk.

## Run it

Open `index.html` in a browser. Nothing to install. It loads Three.js from a CDN.

Controls: WASD or arrows to walk, drag to look, E to interact. Phones and tablets get an on-screen joystick.
